# Fund your wallet

To lock into a course you need **USDC on Solana** in your wallet. There are two
ways to get it, both available right from the deposit screen.

## Option 1 — Pay by card

If your wallet doesn't have enough USDC when you try to lock, the deposit form
shows an **Add funds** button. It opens a card checkout (powered by Stripe,
inside the wallet flow) that delivers USDC straight to your Solana wallet.

- The amount is **prefilled to slightly more than your shortfall** (a small
  buffer above what you're missing, minimum $10) so card and network fees
  don't leave you a few cents short.
- After payment, the USDC lands in your wallet within moments and your balance
  on the deposit screen updates automatically.

<Callout type="warning">
  If you complete a card payment, **wait for your balance to update before
  paying again**. The app guards against accidental double charges — if it
  thinks a payment may already be in flight, it will ask you to confirm before
  opening checkout a second time.
</Callout>

## Option 2 — Send USDC yourself

Already have crypto? Send USDC (Solana network) to your wallet address from any
exchange or wallet. Your address is visible in the wallet section of the app.

<Callout type="error">
  Send **USDC on the Solana network only**. USDC on Ethereum, Base, or any
  other chain will not arrive in your Solana wallet.
</Callout>

## Gas — handled for you

Solana transactions need a tiny amount of SOL for network fees. If your wallet
has none, Locked In automatically sends you a **one-time SOL top-up** (0.005
SOL) the first time you need it, so your first deposit doesn't dead-end on
"insufficient SOL". After that, keep a little SOL around — claiming your funds
at the end needs roughly **0.003 SOL** in fees.
