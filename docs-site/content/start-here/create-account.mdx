# Create your account

Locked In runs in your browser at
[www.lockedin.quest](https://www.lockedin.quest) — no app store, no download.
It's an installable web app: on mobile you can "Add to Home Screen" and it
behaves like a native app.

## Sign in

We use [Privy](https://privy.io) for authentication. Tap **Connect Wallet**
(top-right in the village, or any building when you're not signed in) and pick
one:

- **Email or social login** — Privy creates an **embedded Solana wallet** for
  you automatically. You don't need to know anything about wallets to use
  Locked In; the wallet lives with your login.
- **Your own Solana wallet** — connect Phantom, Solflare, or any supported
  wallet if you already have one.

After sign-in, the backend verifies your wallet with a signed challenge and
issues a session. You'll see your profile and level in the top bar.

<Callout type="info">
  You can wander the village without an account. You only need to sign in when
  you want to do something — enroll, deposit, learn.
</Callout>

## What identifies you

Your **wallet address** is your identity. Leaderboards show a truncated form of
it (like `7fUA…3kQd`). We don't ask for your name.
