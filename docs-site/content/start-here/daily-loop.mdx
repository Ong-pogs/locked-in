# Your daily loop

Once you're locked in, the game is simple: **pass at least one new lesson every
day.**

## What counts as "a day"

Days are measured in **UTC**. A day counts toward your streak (a
"lesson-day") when you pass **at least one lesson you haven't passed before**
during that UTC day.

- Passing means scoring **55 or more out of 100** on the lesson quiz (partial
  credit counts — see [Courses & Lessons](/game-systems/courses-lessons)).
- Re-doing a lesson you already passed does **not** count — the streak rewards
  new progress, not repetition.

<Callout type="warning">
  Because days flip at **midnight UTC**, your local deadline may not be
  midnight. Check what time UTC midnight is where you live and build your
  habit around it.
</Callout>

## What a day of progress does

Each lesson-day:

- **Streak +1** — your streak is the number of consecutive lesson-days.
- **Progress toward a Shield** — every **3 consecutive lesson-days** earns
  you a Shield (you can hold at most 3).
- **XP** — first-time completions award XP toward your level
  (see [XP & Levels](/game-systems/xp-levels)).
- **The flame reflects it** — the flame gauge on your dashboard shows how
  safe your streak currently is.

## What missing a day does

- **With a Shield banked:** the Shield burns, your streak **pauses** (it does
  not reset), and nothing else happens. Shields exist so one bad day doesn't
  wreck a month of work.
- **With no Shields:** your streak resets to 0 and you take a **Lapse**.
  Lapses are the real consequence — they cut the share of yield you'll
  receive at the end: **1 Lapse = you keep 50%, 2 Lapses = you keep 0%** (your
  principal is always safe). Several missed days in a row count as **one**
  Lapse, not one per day.

The full mechanic, with every edge case, is on
[Streak, Shields & Lapses](/game-systems/streak-shields-lapses).
