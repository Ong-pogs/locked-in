# Complete, claim & withdraw

## Completing the course

When you pass the final lesson of the final module, the course is **complete**.
Two things happen immediately:

1. Your course state is **frozen** — no future missed day can touch what
   you've earned. Your streak, your Lapse count, and your yield share are
   locked in at their values the moment you finish.
2. A **claim voucher** is issued for you automatically. The voucher is a
   signed authorization that tells the on-chain program exactly what share of
   yield you're entitled to.

## Claiming

Go to your course's claim page and hit **Claim**. Your wallet signs one
transaction, and the program pays out:

- **Your full principal** — always 100%, regardless of anything.
- **Your yield share** — 100%, 50%, or 0% of the yield your deposit earned,
  depending on your Lapse count
  (see [Lapses & your yield](/your-money/lapses-and-yield)).
- Anything you don't receive from yield goes to the
  [Community Pot](/your-money/community-pot) — it is never kept by us.

Two practical requirements:

<Callout type="warning">
  - **Gas:** claiming needs about **0.003 SOL** in your wallet for network
    fees. The claim page checks this and will tell you if you're short.
  - **Voucher expiry:** each claim voucher is valid for **90 days**. Don't
    finish a course and disappear for four months — claim while it's fresh.
    (If a voucher does expire, a fresh one can be issued from your claim
    page — your funds are not lost.)
</Callout>

## The 180-day safety valve

If a lock ever sits unresolved for **180 days** after it was created, the
program allows the funds to be returned to the owner — permissionlessly,
meaning anyone can trigger it, and the funds can only go to **you**. This
exists so that no bug, outage, or disappearance of Locked In itself can strand
your money. Details in
[Claiming & force return](/your-money/claiming-force-return).

## After claiming

The USDC (principal + yield share) is in your wallet — it's yours, do what you
like. The completed course unlocks **Practice Mode** forever (see
[Practice Mode](/game-systems/practice-mode)), and you can lock into your next
course whenever you're ready.
