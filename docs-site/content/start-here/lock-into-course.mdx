# Lock into a course

Head to the **Academy** (the big building on the left of the village) to browse
courses. Each course shows its modules and lessons up front — you can see
exactly what you're committing to before you lock anything.

## The deposit

When you choose a course, you pick how much USDC to lock:

| Rule | Value (beta) |
|---|---|
| Minimum per course | **$10** |
| Maximum per course | **$50** |
| Platform-wide cap | **$1,000** total locked across all users |
| Quick presets | $10 / $25 / $50 |

These are **beta caps enforced on-chain** — the program itself rejects
deposits outside them, and will tell you if the platform cap would be
exceeded.

## There is no timer

Your lock has **no fixed duration**. It releases when you **complete the
course** — that's the whole deal. Finish in two weeks, get your money in two
weeks. There's also a hard safety valve: if a lock ever sits unresolved for
**180 days**, the funds can be force-returned to you regardless of anything
else. See [Claiming & force return](/your-money/claiming-force-return).

## What signing means

Locking is a real Solana transaction that you sign with your wallet:

1. You approve the transfer of your chosen USDC amount into the course's
   **on-chain vault account** (owned by the Locked In program, not by us — see
   [Custody](/your-money/custody)).
2. Before your first deposit you'll be asked to accept the terms and risk
   disclosure. This is recorded once, and it's a condition of using the
   platform.
3. The backend confirms your lock **from the chain itself** — your enrollment
   is driven by what's actually on-chain, not by what the app claims.

<Callout type="info">
  One lock per course. Once you complete a course you can't re-lock into it —
  completed means done. You can lock into a different course any time.
</Callout>
