# The Community Pot

The Community Pot (the **Tavern** in the village) is where redirected yield
goes — and where it comes back out, monthly, to the people who kept their
streaks.

## Money in

Whenever someone claims with Lapses, the yield share they forfeited
(50% or 100% of their net yield) is booked to the pot at settlement, on-chain.
The pot is funded **only** by redirected yield — never by principal, and never
topped up with house money that could distort incentives.

## Money out — the monthly distribution

Once a month, the previous **full UTC calendar month** is closed and the pot
accumulated in that window is distributed.

**Eligibility** — at the time the window closes you need:

- an **active lock** (real principal, on-chain, still locked), and
- a **current streak greater than 0**.

**Your share** is proportional to your weight:

```
weight = your locked principal × your current streak
```

A $50 lock with a 30-day streak weighs six times as much as a $50 lock with a
5-day streak — the pot rewards sustained consistency, scaled by skin in the
game. Payouts are calculated so the window's pot is fully distributed with no
rounding leftovers lost.

<Callout type="info">
  Completing a course doesn't disqualify you from a window you were active in
  — and every payout is a real on-chain USDC transfer you can verify.
</Callout>

## Seeing it

The Tavern shows the current pot size, the next distribution window, and past
distributions. The [Leaderboard](/game-systems/leaderboard) shows recent pot
payouts per wallet.
