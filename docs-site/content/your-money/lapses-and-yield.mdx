# Lapses & your yield

This page is the money view of the
[Streak, Shields & Lapses](/game-systems/streak-shields-lapses) mechanic.

## The split

Your Lapse count at course completion sets your yield share — checked and
enforced by the on-chain program at claim time:

| Lapses | You keep | Community Pot receives |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | 100% of net yield | 0% |
| 1 | 50% | 50% |
| 2+ | 0% | 100% |

"Net yield" is gross yield minus the platform fee (currently **0%**, so today
net = gross).

## The guarantees

- **Principal first.** The settlement math pays your principal back in full
  before any split happens. Lapses cannot touch it.
- **Frozen at completion.** The count that matters is your Lapse count when
  you pass the final lesson. After that, nothing — not even months of
  inactivity before claiming — can change it.
- **Nothing goes to us.** Redirected yield goes to the
  [Community Pot](/your-money/community-pot) and is distributed to other
  learners. Locked In's cut is the fee (0% in beta), nothing else.

<Callout type="info">
  The system is designed so that the *platform* never benefits from your
  failure. If you lapse, your yield funds the people who didn't — that keeps
  our incentives pointed at helping you finish.
</Callout>
