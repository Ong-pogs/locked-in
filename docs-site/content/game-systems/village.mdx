# The Village

The village is Locked In's home screen — a painted scene where each building is
a door to part of the app. Hover (or tap) a building to see its name light up.

## The buildings

| Building | Where it takes you |
|---|---|
| **Academy** — the big tudor on the left | Browse and enroll in [courses](/game-systems/courses-lessons) |
| **Notice board** | The [Leaderboard](/game-systems/leaderboard) |
| **Tavern** — right of center | The [Community Pot](/your-money/community-pot): current pot size and monthly distributions |
| **Practice Hall** — the blue-lit chapel | [Practice Mode](/game-systems/practice-mode): replay lessons from courses you've completed |

The other buildings are scenery (for now).

## The dashboard

Your **dashboard** isn't a building — it's always one tap away via the profile
button and level badge in the top bar. It shows your active courses, streak,
Shields, Lapses, the [flame](/game-systems/flame), locked balances, and your
activity log.

## Wander first

You can explore the village signed out. Any building that needs an account will
ask you to connect when you tap it — not before.
