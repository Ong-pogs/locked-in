# Streak, Shields & Lapses

This is the core mechanic of Locked In. Everything — the flame, the
leaderboard, your yield — hangs off these three numbers.

## Streak

Your **streak** is the count of consecutive **lesson-days**: UTC days on which
you passed at least one lesson you hadn't passed before.

- Pass a new lesson today (UTC) → streak +1 for that day.
- Extra lessons the same day are great for finishing faster, but the streak
  counts **days**, not lessons.

## Shields

Shields are your safety net. They absorb missed days so that real life doesn't
destroy your progress.

| Rule | Value |
|---|---|
| Maximum Shields held | **3** |
| Earning rate | **+1 Shield per 3 consecutive lesson-days** |
| Miss a day with a Shield | Shield burns, streak **pauses** — no reset, no Lapse |
| Can you buy Shields? | **No.** Shields are only earned by learning |

"Pauses" means exactly that: a 12-day streak that hits a shielded miss stays
a 12-day streak, and continues to 13 when you return.

## Lapses

A **Lapse** is what happens when you miss a day with **no Shields left**:

- Your streak resets to **0**.
- Your Lapse count goes up by 1 — permanently, for this course.
- Your yield share drops (see the table below).

Two rules soften this:

1. **Consecutive missed days merge into one Lapse.** If you disappear for a
   week with no Shields, that's *one* Lapse, not seven. The Lapse "closes"
   when you come back and pass a lesson; only a later, separate absence can
   open a new one.
2. **Lapses stop mattering at 2.** The count is capped — after two Lapses
   your yield share is already 0% and can't go lower. Your principal is
   untouchable regardless.

## What Lapses cost you

| Lapses | Yield you keep at claim | Where the rest goes |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | **100%** | — |
| 1 | **50%** | [Community Pot](/your-money/community-pot) |
| 2+ | **0%** | Community Pot |

<Callout type="info">
  **Your principal is never touched.** The worst case, ever, is receiving
  exactly what you deposited (subject to
  [strategy risk](/your-money/risks)) with zero yield. Locked In never takes
  principal as a penalty.
</Callout>

## Who judges a missed day

A daily sweep runs shortly after **00:30 UTC** and evaluates the previous day
for every active lock: lesson-day, shielded miss, or Lapse. The current UTC day
is never judged early — you have until midnight UTC, always.

## Completion freezes everything

The moment you complete a course, its streak/Shield/Lapse state is **frozen**.
A completed course can never gain a Lapse — your earned yield share is safe
even if you take a long break before claiming.
