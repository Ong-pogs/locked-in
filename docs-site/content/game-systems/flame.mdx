# The Flame

The flame on your dashboard is a **mirror of your streak's health** — a
single glance tells you how safe you are. It's derived entirely from your
Shields, Lapses, and whether you've learned today; there's nothing to feed or
maintain beyond doing your lessons.

## Flame states

| State | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Blazing** | You've passed a lesson today and hold a full **3 Shields**. Fully protected. |
| **Flickering** | You're active but not fully shielded — you have progress to protect and fewer than 3 Shields banked. |
| **Dark** | You've taken **1 Lapse**. Half your yield share is gone; the other half is on the line. |
| **Extinguished** | **2+ Lapses.** Your yield share is 0%. Your principal remains safe, and finishing the course still returns it in full. |

## Reading it honestly

The flame never lies in your favor: if it's blazing, a missed day tomorrow
costs you a Shield, not a Lapse. If it's dark, the message is "one more
unprotected miss and the remaining yield is gone." Use it as your daily
go/no-go signal.
