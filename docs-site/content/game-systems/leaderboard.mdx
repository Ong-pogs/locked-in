# Leaderboard

The **notice board** in the village shows the community leaderboard — who's
holding the longest streaks right now.

## Ranking

Entries are ranked by:

1. **Current streak** (longest first) — your best current streak across your
   actively locked courses.
2. **Total locked principal** as the tiebreak.

Each row shows a truncated wallet address, streak length and status, number of
active courses, total USDC locked, and — when a
[Community Pot](/your-money/community-pot) window has closed — that wallet's
most recent pot payout.

## Freshness

The leaderboard is a **snapshot**, refreshed on a schedule (not per
page-load), with the current pot size and the next distribution window shown
alongside. Your own row is highlighted wherever you rank.

## Why it matters

The leaderboard isn't just vanity: the same streak that ranks you here is what
makes you eligible for the monthly Community Pot distribution, and your
**streak × locked principal** determines your share of it.
