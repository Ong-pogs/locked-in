# Courses & Lessons

## Structure

Courses are made of **modules**, and modules are made of **lessons**. A lesson
is a piece of teaching content followed by a graded quiz. You can always see
the full course outline — every module and lesson — before you lock in.

<Callout type="info">
  Lessons require an active lock. You can browse any course's outline freely,
  but starting a lesson requires having USDC locked behind that course —
  that's the point of the platform.
</Callout>

## The quiz

Lessons end with a mix of question types:

- **Multiple choice** — graded instantly.
- **Short answer** — free-text answers graded automatically against a rubric,
  with **partial credit**. You don't need to parrot exact wording; you need to
  demonstrate you understood.

### Instant feedback

Each question can be **checked as you answer it** — you get immediate
right/wrong feedback per question. Once checked, an answer is locked; you
can't fish for the right option by re-checking.

### Passing

| Rule | Value |
|---|---|
| Pass mark | **55 / 100** |
| Partial credit | Yes, on short-answer questions |
| Attempts | Failing is not punished — take the lesson again |

A failed attempt doesn't cost you anything except time: only **passing** a
lesson for the first time advances your course and your streak. There is no
penalty for retrying.

## Quick Recall

After you complete a course, lessons offer a **Quick Recall** check — a fast,
no-stakes quiz to test whether the material stuck. Quick Recall never affects
your progress, streak, or yield.

## What counts toward your streak

Only **first-time passes** count as streak progress. Replaying a passed lesson
(in [Practice Mode](/game-systems/practice-mode) or otherwise) is graded and
shows you results, but writes nothing to your progress, streak, or XP.
