# FAQ & Troubleshooting

## Money

**Can I lose my deposit?**
Locked In never takes your principal as a penalty — the worst the consequence
system can do is redirect your *yield*. The remaining risks to principal are
the underlying ones every DeFi deposit has (smart-contract and strategy risk)
— read [Risks](/your-money/risks) before depositing.

**Can I withdraw early / quit a course?**
No. That's the product: the lock releases on **course completion**, not on
request. The only other exit is the [180-day force
return](/your-money/claiming-force-return). Don't lock money you might need
next week.

**Why is the maximum only $50?**
Beta caps, enforced on-chain. They bound everyone's risk while the system is
young and will lift as it matures.

**Where does my lost yield go if I lapse?**
To the [Community Pot](/your-money/community-pot), distributed monthly to
active learners with live streaks. Never to us.

**What's the APY?**
Whatever Kamino's USDC lending market pays — shown live in the app, floating,
and [not guaranteed](/your-money/yield-apy).

## Streaks

**What time does the day reset?**
**Midnight UTC**, everywhere. Work out your local equivalent and set a
reminder well before it.

**I missed a day — how bad is it?**
Had a Shield? It burned, your streak paused, nothing else happened. No
Shields? Streak reset + a [Lapse](/game-systems/streak-shields-lapses): first
Lapse halves your yield share, second zeroes it. Principal untouched either
way.

**I was away a whole week — is that 7 lapses?**
No. Consecutive missed days merge into **one** Lapse. Coming back always
makes sense.

**Can I buy back a Shield or clear a Lapse?**
No. Shields are earned only by learning (1 per 3 consecutive lesson-days, max
3); Lapses are permanent for that course. There is nothing to buy in Locked
In — the only currency is showing up.

**Does finishing lessons faster help my streak?**
It finishes the *course* faster (fewer days at risk!), but the streak counts
days, not lessons — one new passed lesson per day is what it needs.

## Claiming

**My claim says I need SOL.**
Claiming costs about **0.003 SOL** in network fees. Top up a little SOL —
your one-time starter stipend was 0.005 SOL, and card-funding USDC doesn't
add SOL.

**My voucher expired.**
Vouchers last **90 days**, but an expired voucher doesn't forfeit anything —
open your claim page and a fresh one will be issued.

**The transaction failed.**
Almost always: not enough SOL, or a congested network. Check your SOL
balance, wait a moment, retry. Your funds never move on a failed transaction.

## Account

**I lost access to my login.**
Your funds are tied to your **wallet**, not your session. Email/social logins
recover through Privy's standard recovery. Self-custodied wallets: your seed
phrase is the recovery — we cannot restore it for you.

**Is my name shown anywhere?**
No — leaderboards show truncated wallet addresses only.
