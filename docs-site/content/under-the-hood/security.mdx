# Trust & security

The high-level answers to "why should I trust this with money?"

## You can't cheat the learning

- All grading happens on our servers — answer keys never reach your browser.
- Answers lock once checked, so options can't be brute-forced.
- Replays and practice runs are clearly separated and never count toward
  streaks or yield.

## We can't touch your principal

- The vault is owned by an on-chain program with no "withdraw to admin"
  ability. The only destination your principal can ever have is **your
  wallet**.
- The worst our servers could ever get wrong is the *yield percentage* on a
  claim — never the principal.

## The blast radius is capped

- Beta limits ($50 per lock, $1,000 platform-wide) are enforced by the
  blockchain itself, bounding what could ever be at risk while the system is
  young.
- If Locked In vanished tomorrow, the **180-day force return** still gets
  everyone their funds back — it needs nothing from us to work.

## Found a problem?

If you believe you've found a security issue, please reach out before making
it public — responsible disclosure will be respected and credited. Contact
links are at [www.lockedin.quest](https://www.lockedin.quest).
