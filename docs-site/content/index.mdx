# What is Locked In?

**Locked In is a learn-to-earn platform on Solana.** You lock real USDC behind a
course. To get it back — plus the yield it earns while locked — you have to
actually finish the course, one lesson a day.

The idea is simple and a little uncomfortable: promises to "start learning
tomorrow" are free, so we break them. A locked deposit isn't free. Loss
aversion is the strongest motivator behavioral science has found, so Locked In
puts it to work **for** you instead of against you.

## The loop

1. **Lock** — pick a course and lock USDC into an on-chain vault
   ($10–$50 per course during beta).
2. **Learn** — complete at least one lesson every day. Lessons are graded;
   you need a real passing score, not a pulse.
3. **Keep your streak** — daily learning builds a streak and earns you
   Shields that protect it. Miss days without protection and you take
   **Lapses** that cut the yield you'll receive.
4. **Complete & claim** — finish the course and claim your full principal
   back, plus your share of the yield it earned.

## What happens to your money

- Your USDC sits in a **program-owned vault on Solana** — not in our bank
  account. See [Custody](/your-money/custody).
- While locked, it's deposited into a **lending strategy (Kamino)** to earn
  yield. See [Yield & APY](/your-money/yield-apy).
- **Your principal is never taken as a penalty.** Ever. The only thing at
  stake from missing days is the *yield* — miss enough and your yield share
  goes to the [Community Pot](/your-money/community-pot), which is paid out
  monthly to the people who kept their streaks.

<Callout type="info">
  Locked In is in **beta**. Deposits are capped at $50 per course and $1,000
  across the whole platform while we harden the system. Caps will lift as the
  beta progresses.
</Callout>
