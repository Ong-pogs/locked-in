## Section 2: The One Thing You Need to Understand

LockedIn uses digital dollars.

Not Bitcoin. Not some volatile coin that could crash overnight. Digital dollars. They're called USDC, and here's all you need to know:

**1 USDC = $1. Always.**

That's it. It's a digital version of a US dollar. It doesn't go up. It doesn't go down. $50 of USDC today is $50 of USDC tomorrow, next week, next month.

USDC is issued by a company called Circle. It's backed by real US dollars sitting in real bank accounts. It's used by millions of people worldwide. It's not experimental -- it's just dollars on the internet.

## Section 3: How LockedIn Works

You lock money to lock in your commitment.

1. You lock some USDC (say $50) for a set time (say 30 days)
2. You learn every day -- complete lessons, build a streak
3. Your locked money earns yield (interest) while it sits there
4. If you stay consistent -> you get your money back + rewards
5. If you slack off -> your earned rewards start shrinking

Stay consistent and your money is safe. Show up, do your lessons, and you get everything back plus rewards. But if you start slacking -- you'll lose the yield (interest) your money has been earning, because it gets redirected to the community pot instead of coming to you. The good news: your principal is never touched and your lock period never gets extended. The only thing on the line is the interest you could have earned.

Think of it as a promise to yourself -- LockedIn just makes sure you keep it.

Think of it like a gym membership, but reversed:
- **Normal gym:** You pay, and if you don't go, you lose money.
- **LockedIn:** You lock money, and if you consistently learn, you'll earn money.

## Section 5: Locking Your Commitment

Choose your course. Set your stakes.

1. Pick a course -- what do you want to learn?
2. Set your amount -- how much USDC do you want to lock? (minimum: $X)
3. Set your duration -- 30, 60, or 90 days

**What happens when you tap "Lock":**
- Your USDC moves from your account into a smart contract (automatic code on the blockchain)
- It stays there for the duration you chose
- It earns yield (interest) while it sits there
- At the end, you get your USDC back + any rewards you've earned

**"Is this safe?"**
Yes. Your USDC isn't in LockedIn's bank account. It's locked in transparent, automatic code that nobody -- not even us -- can tamper with.
