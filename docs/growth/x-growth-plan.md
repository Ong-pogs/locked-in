# X Growth Plan: @LockedIn_Real

> Status: draft for team review (2026-09-29). Owner: Marcus.
>
> This is a marketing plan, not a code spec. The product ideas near the end are ideas only.
> Each one needs its own design and sign-off before anyone builds it.

## Goal

- Grow [@LockedIn_Real](https://x.com/LockedIn_Real) from 29 to at least 100 followers.
- Post things with real substance, not generic motivation.
- Use X to drive signups for the Founding 100 launch ([PR #3](https://github.com/Ong-pogs/locked-in/pull/3)).

Rough estimate: about 8 weeks at 15 minutes a day. A strong launch with Superteam MY reposts
could make it faster. This is a guess, not a promise.

## Starting point (2026-09-29)

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Followers | 29 |
| Following | 27 |
| Posts | 81 |
| Posting pace | About 1 a week |
| Likes per recent post | 3 to 5 |

What we found:

- **Generic posts.** Recent posts are short motivation lines ("One day, or day one"). They sound
  like every other motivation account.
- **Wrong audience.** The bio sells "daily habits". The site sells "Learn Solana. Lock deposits.
  Stay consistent." A habits fan clicks through and only sees "Connect your wallet".
- **The best idea was hidden.** Earlier content rules banned crypto talk. That removed what makes
  LockedIn different: you put USDC behind a course, and falling off costs you.
- **Weak link previews.** The live site shares a 512px square icon where X expects a wide image.
  PR #3 adds a proper preview image.
- **No explanation for new visitors.** Every button on the live site says "Connect your wallet".
  PR #3 adds the Founding 100 landing section.

## Copy rules (both accounts, every post)

1. Never use these words: "guaranteed", "risk free", "every cent back", "learn to earn".
   It is the same list the PR #3 launch checks block on the site.
2. No promises about returns, yield rates, or getting a deposit back.
3. Use the site's words: "Founding 100", "shields", "Stop collecting courses. Finish one."
4. Any post about locking USDC mentions the capped beta or points to the risks page.
5. Never show a learner's dollar amounts or wallet address.
6. Ong checks the facts in build-in-public posts before they go out.

## Daily routine (15 minutes)

- **2 min:** Check the scheduled post went out. Answer anyone who replied.
- **10 min:** Write 5 to 8 real replies. Use an X List of Solana builders and Superteam accounts,
  and search for people posting "learning Solana" or "day 1 of Rust". Each reply adds a tip or a
  question. No "great post!" and no links.
- **3 min:** Follow back real learners and answer mentions.

People posting "learning Solana" are exactly our future users. Replying to them grows followers
and signups at the same time.

## Launch plan

### Stage 1: Warm-up (starts now)

- Brand account: the weekly content and the daily routine.
- Ong: 3 posts a week (see Ong's playbook).
- Once a week, post: "Founding 100 is coming. Reply 'in' and we'll DM you the day it opens."
  Keep a list of everyone who replies.
- Every follower gained now will see the launch thread later.

### Stage 2: Launch kit (when PR #3 merges)

Launch date: when Marcus and Ong agree PR #3 is ready to merge. Pick a date 3 days out.

1. **3 days before:** teaser post, for example "How many unfinished Solana courses are in your
   bookmarks?"
2. **Launch day:** post the thread below and pin it. Ong quote-posts it with a personal line.
   Ask Superteam MY to repost. DM everyone on the "in" list.
3. **Days 2 to 7:** one post a day. Spots filled, a 30-second demo video of the fire and the
   Brewer, and the first learner shoutouts.

Launch thread draft (Ong confirms post 3 matches how mainnet works):

> 1/ Most people don't need another Solana course. They need to finish one.
> Today we're opening LockedIn to 100 founding learners. 🧵

> 2/ Free courses are easy to start and free to quit. So we quit.
> Bookmarks full. Skills empty.

> 3/ How it works: lock $10 to $50 USDC behind a Solana course. Finish the work to unlock your position.
> Fall off after your shields are gone, and part of your yield goes to learners who stayed consistent.

> 4/ What you should know: this is a capped mainnet beta. Yield is variable. The software is unaudited.
> Your funds face smart-contract, Kamino, USDC, and Solana risks. Read the risks page first.

> 5/ Founding 100 is open: lockedin.quest
> Joining doesn't reserve deposit capacity. Backed by @superteamMY

Post 4 is on purpose. With real money, being upfront about risk builds trust. It also matches
the disclosures on the site.

### Stage 3: After launch

Keep the weekly rhythm. The Friday post becomes the Founding 100 counter plus learner shoutouts.

## Ong's playbook

**Role:** The brand account says what LockedIn is. Ong says why he built it and how.
Founder posts feel human and usually grow faster than brand posts.

**Routine:** 3 posts a week, plus quote-posts of the brand's big posts. Marcus sends drafts in
the Sunday batch. Ong rewrites them in his own voice and posts.

**Post types:**

- **Origin story:** why he built LockedIn. Only Ong can write the true details.
- **Technical deep dives:** these land better from a person than from a brand.

  > Our Solana program cost ~15 SOL to deploy.
  > I merged our programs into one and switched to a release build.
  > Now it's ~2.5 SOL. Count how many programs you actually need.

- **Honest founder moments** (Ong adds the real reason):

  > I renamed our Arena to "Clockwork Spire."
  > Less than a day later, I renamed it back.
  > Naming things is still the hardest problem in software.

- **Launch day:** a personal "why this matters to me" quote-post of the launch thread.

**His one rule:** the copy rules above apply to his account too.

## Learner shoutouts

- Until the share card exists, Marcus DMs learners who hit day 7 or 14: "Want a shoutout on X?"
- Format: "Day 14 for @handle. Finished Anchor basics. 2 shields left."
- Only the handle, day count, and progress. Never amounts or wallet addresses.

## Product ideas (not approved for build)

1. **Ship PR #3 first.** It gives X visitors a real landing page and a proper link preview.
2. **Share-your-streak card.** At day 3, 7, 14, and 30, learners get a "Share on X" button.
   The image shows the day count, the course, and the fire, and tags @LockedIn_Real.
   No dollar amounts, for privacy and to stay clear of money claims.
3. **Arena duel invite.** A "challenge a friend to a Solana quiz duel" link with a preview card
   that clearly says a stake is needed. After the match, the winner can share a result card.
4. **Public stats line.** "N learners locked in, M lessons finished this week" on the landing page.
   Show it only once the numbers look healthy (say 20+ learners). It doubles as the Friday post.

Each one puts @LockedIn_Real in front of the sharer's followers, who are likely learners too.
They build on PR #3, so they wait until it merges.

## How we measure

Every Sunday, write down:

- followers
- profile visits
- link clicks to lockedin.quest (PR #3 adds click tracking)
- replies received
- Founding 100 signups (after launch)

If growth stalls for 2 weeks, check which post types got the most replies and do more of those.
